## 📁 Featured project

### ⛽ [Fuel Price Analysis and CPI Correlation, Nairobi (Feb 2024 to Feb 2025)](https://github.com/kygosloth/fuel-cpi-correlation)

I wanted to see how fuel prices in Nairobi move with the exchange rate, world crude prices and CPI, so I pulled the data together and analysed it with correlation and regression.

- Super Petrol, Diesel and Kerosene prices move almost together. Super Petrol correlates **0.96** with both Diesel and Kerosene.
- Super Petrol also tracks Murban crude (**0.86**) and the exchange rate (**0.74**).
- Against Transport and Energy CPI, fuel prices showed an inverse relationship over this period.
- My regression estimated that a KSh 5 rise in fuel prices goes with a **0.48-point fall in CPI** (R² = 0.91).
- I also looked at what could help: monitoring subsidies and policy to steady CPI, and hedging or diversifying fuel imports to soften exchange rate swings.

**Tools:** Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn

[📂 Repository](https://github.com/kygosloth/fuel-cpi-correlation) · [🌐 Full write-up](https://erick-kigo-portfolio.vercel.app/projects.html)
